# Call With A Photo - Free Idea

## I have an idea. Free. For elderly people living alone.

They can no longer dial a number.
They forget where they put their phone.
They can no longer plug in a charger.
So they stay alone, without calling.

My proposal is simple:

### 1. One app, installed once
One app, installed once, at the beginning, by a loved one or by the seller.
This app disables everything else on the phone. No menus, no contacts list, no settings to touch by mistake.
The phone becomes only one thing: a phone to call with a photo.
And this app is always ready. As soon as the phone is shaken, even with a small movement, it launches by itself and turns on the camera.

### 2. Flash turns on automatically
As soon as the app feels that the phone is taken in a hand, or as soon as it detects a part of the QR code, the flash turns on automatically.
No need to press anything. Even in the dark, the code is lit.

### 3. Photos on the wall
We put on the wall, near their bed or their favorite chair, large photos of their loved ones.
Under each photo, a QR code.

### 4. To call
The person points the phone at the photo. The call starts by itself, even if only part of the QR code is seen.

### 5. Gentle voice if not well aimed
If the code is not well aimed, a gentle voice, recorded beforehand, kindly asks the person to try again.
Not an error message. Not a beep. A soft, familiar voice saying: try again, you are almost there.

### 6. To hang up
The loved one hangs up. The elderly person has nothing to do.

### 7. Pouch + Short cable + Charging hidden inside
The phone stays in a special pouch, attached by a SHORT cable to one single fixed place.
Inside this cable, the charging cable is hidden.
A small smart switch stops charging when it is full.
No loss. No plugging. No risk of falling with a long cable.

## This can also be used by a category of people with disabilities.

People with disabilities who live alone at home face the same difficulties: they cannot dial a number easily, they cannot plug in a charger, they cannot remember where they put their phone, and they cannot hang up by themselves.

With this same system - one app, photos on the wall, a phone in a pouch with a short cable, and a loved one who hangs up - they can also call their family by themselves, without waiting for someone to come and help them. They keep their dignity, their independence, and their link with their loved ones.

## My intention

I want no price for this. I just want to serve these vulnerable people, for free.

If you work in tech, in care, in an association, and this idea can help someone where you live, take it. Build it. Improve it.

It is yours.
